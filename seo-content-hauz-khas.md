*   **Benefit:** Provides the highest level of control and success for most couples.

### IUI Treatment (Intrauterine Insemination)
A simpler, less invasive option where we place concentrated sperm directly into the uterus during ovulation. It’s often a great first step for younger couples.
*   **Suitable for:** Mild male infertility or cervical issues.
*   **Benefit:** Cost-effective and requires minimal medication.
**[Is IUI right for you?]**

### ICSI Treatment
For cases where sperm motility or count is an issue, we use ICSI to inject a single, healthy sperm directly into the egg. This is a game-changer for male factor infertility.
*   **Suitable for:** Low sperm count or previous fertilization failure.
*   **Benefit:** Drastically improves fertilization rates in difficult cases.
