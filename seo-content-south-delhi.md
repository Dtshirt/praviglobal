# SECTION 4 — TREATMENTS WE OFFER

## Fertility Treatments That Actually Make Sense for You

### In Vitro Fertilization (IVF)
**What it is:** The most effective fertility treatment available today. We retrieve your eggs, combine them with sperm in our lab, and place a healthy embryo back into your uterus.
**Who it's for:** Couples dealing with blocked tubes, severe endometriosis, or situations where IUI hasn't worked.
**The benefit:** It gives us the highest amount of control over the fertilization process, leading to the best pregnancy rates.
**CTA:** [Ask about our IVF process]

### IUI (Intrauterine Insemination)
**What it is:** A simpler, less invasive starting point. We wash and concentrate the sperm, placing it directly into the uterus exactly when you ovulate.
**Who it's for:** Younger couples, those with mild sperm issues, or cases where we just can't find a specific reason for the infertility.
**The benefit:** It’s much more affordable than IVF and requires very little medication.
**CTA:** [See if IUI is right for you]

### ICSI (Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection)
**What it is:** An add-on to IVF. Instead of letting sperm find the egg on their own, our embryologist selects the single best-looking sperm and injects it directly into the egg.
**Who it's for:** Men with very low sperm counts, poor motility, or abnormal sperm shapes.
**The benefit:** It almost guarantees fertilization, even in severe male infertility cases.
**CTA:** [Learn about ICSI]

### Egg Freezing
**What it is:** A way to hit pause. We retrieve your eggs now while they are young and healthy, and freeze them safely for whenever you're ready.
**Who it's for:** Women focusing on their careers, those who haven't found the right partner yet, or women facing medical treatments like chemotherapy.
**The benefit:** It takes the pressure off your biological clock.
**CTA:** [Get details on egg freezing]

### Male Infertility Care
**What it is:** Sometimes, the sperm just needs a little help. We offer everything from basic semen analysis to minor surgical procedures (like TESA) to find healthy sperm.
**Who it's for:** Men who have been told they have a low count or zero sperm.
**The benefit:** It allows men with severe fertility issues to still have biological children.

### PCOS Management & Pregnancy
**What it is:** PCOS makes ovulation unpredictable. We use specific, careful medication to help your body release an egg safely.
**Who it's for:** Women with irregular periods, high androgens, or diagnosed polycystic ovaries.

# SECTION 5 — SUCCESS RATES

## Let's Talk Honestly About IVF Success Rates

If a clinic promises you a "100% guarantee," you should walk away. Biology doesn't work like that. 

At our South Delhi centre, our success rates are consistently higher than the national average, but your personal chance of taking a baby home depends heavily on your age and your egg quality. 

Generally speaking:
*   **Under 35:** You have the highest chance of success, often reaching up to 70% per cycle.
*   **35 to 37:** Rates are still very strong, usually hovering around 55% to 60%.
*   **38 and Over:** The egg quality starts to drop, so success rates decrease. However, with custom protocols and sometimes genetic testing of the embryos, we help many older women conceive safely.

We focus on transferring a single, highly graded "blastocyst" (a 5-day-old embryo). This greatly improves your chances of getting pregnant while minimizing the risk of twins or triplets, which can be tough on your body.

# SECTION 9 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

## Common Questions We Hear Every Day

**1. Is the IVF process going to hurt?**
Honestly, no. The injections use needles that are as thin as an eyelash, much like an insulin pen. The egg retrieval is done while you are asleep under light anesthesia, so you won't feel a thing. You might have some mild period-like cramps afterward, but that's usually it.

**2. How long does a whole IVF cycle take?**
From the time you start your period to the day we transfer the embryo, it takes about 3 to 4 weeks. 

**3. Will I need to quit my job or go on bed rest?**
Not at all. You will need to come in for a few short ultrasound appointments, but you can go to work normally. Even after the embryo transfer, we don't recommend complete bed rest. Light, normal daily activity is actually better for blood flow.

**4. I have PCOS. Does that mean IVF won't work?**
Actually, it’s the opposite. Women with PCOS usually have a lot of eggs. The challenge is getting them to grow safely without overstimulating your ovaries. Our doctors are experts at managing PCOS during IVF, and these patients usually have excellent pregnancy rates.

**5. How much does an IVF cycle usually cost here?**
It varies depending on your medication needs, but we promise complete transparency. Book a consultation, and once we see your scan, we will give you the exact figure down to the rupee.

**6. What's the difference between IVF and IUI?**
Think of IUI as giving the sperm a head start; we place it directly into the uterus, but fertilization still happens naturally inside your body. With IVF, we take the eggs out, fertilize them in our lab, and put a growing embryo back inside.

**7. Can we choose the gender of the baby?**
No. In India, gender selection is strictly illegal. We focus entirely on making sure you have a healthy baby, regardless of gender.

**8. What happens if we get more than one good embryo?**
That’s a great scenario! We will transfer the best one, and we can freeze the rest. If you want another child in a few years, or if the first transfer doesn't work, we can use the frozen embryos without you having to do the injections again.
