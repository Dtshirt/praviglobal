### IUI Treatment (Intrauterine Insemination)
**Introduction:** A highly effective, minimally invasive first-line fertility treatment.
**Indications:** Mild male factor infertility, cervical mucus issues, or unexplained infertility.
**Benefits:** Cost-effective, less invasive than IVF, and requires minimal medication.
**Procedure Overview:** Concentrated, highly motile sperm are washed and placed directly into the uterus during ovulation.
**Who Needs It:** Younger couples with unexplained infertility or women using donor sperm.
**Success Possibilities:** Good success rates for younger patients when combined with ovulation induction.
**CTA:** [Learn More About IUI]

### ICSI Treatment (Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection)
**Introduction:** A specialized form of IVF that revolutionizes the treatment of severe male infertility.
**Indications:** Low sperm count, poor sperm morphology (shape), or low motility.
**Benefits:** Allows fertilization even with a very small number of viable sperm.
**Procedure Overview:** A single, healthy sperm is meticulously selected and injected directly into the center of the egg using a microscopic needle.
**Who Needs It:** Couples where the male partner has severe sperm abnormalities or previous IVF cycles have failed to fertilize.
**Success Possibilities:** Drastically improves fertilization rates compared to conventional IVF.
**CTA:** [Discuss ICSI with Our Experts]

# SECTION 5 — IVF PROCESS STEP BY STEP

## The IVF Process: A Simple Step-by-Step Guide

We believe in making your fertility journey as transparent and stress-free as possible. Here is what you can expect during your IVF cycle:

1.  **Initial Consultation:** Your journey begins with a detailed discussion with our IVF expert in Lajpat Nagar. We review your medical history, previous tests, and lifestyle to understand your unique situation.
2.  **Comprehensive Fertility Tests:** Both partners undergo specific blood tests, hormonal evaluations, and a 3D transvaginal ultrasound to map out the best treatment protocol.
3.  **Ovarian Stimulation:** To retrieve multiple eggs, hormonal injections are administered for 10-12 days. We monitor your progress closely with frequent ultrasounds and blood tests.
4.  **Egg Retrieval:** A minor, painless day-care procedure performed under light anesthesia. The doctor uses ultrasound guidance to gently extract the mature eggs.
5.  **Fertilization in the Lab:** On the same day, the eggs are combined with sperm in our advanced lab (via standard IVF or ICSI) to create embryos.
6.  **Embryo Culture & Transfer:** The embryos develop in our specialized incubators for 3-5 days. The healthiest embryo (usually a Day-5 blastocyst) is then gently transferred into the uterus via a thin catheter.
7.  **The Pregnancy Test:** Approximately two weeks after the embryo transfer, a Beta-hCG blood test is conducted to confirm the much-awaited positive pregnancy result!

# SECTION 11 — FAQ SECTION

## Frequently Asked Questions About IVF in Lajpat Nagar

**1. Which is the best IVF centre in Lajpat Nagar?**
Our clinic is widely recognized as a top fertility centre due to our advanced IVF lab, experienced specialists, ethical practices, and consistently high success rates in complex infertility cases.

**2. What is the average IVF treatment cost in Delhi?**
The cost varies based on the individual protocol, medications needed, and advanced techniques (like ICSI or PGT). We offer transparent, affordable pricing with EMI options. Contact us for a detailed breakdown.

**3. Is the IVF procedure painful?**
No, IVF is generally not painful. The daily hormone injections use very fine needles, similar to insulin pens. The egg retrieval is done under short, light anesthesia, so you feel no pain during the procedure.

**4. What is the true IVF success rate?**
Success rates depend heavily on the woman's age and the cause of infertility. Generally, women under 35 have up to a 65-75% chance of success per cycle at our advanced facility.

**5. Can PCOS patients get pregnant through IVF?**
Absolutely. In fact, women with PCOS often have excellent IVF success rates because they produce a high number of eggs. We use specialized protocols to ensure safety and prevent OHSS.

**6. How many IVF cycles are typically needed?**
Many patients succeed in their first cycle. However, on average, some couples may need 2 to 3 cycles. The cumulative success rate increases with multiple attempts.

**7. Is IVF safe for the mother and baby?**
Yes, IVF is a very safe, globally standardized medical procedure. Millions of healthy babies have been born worldwide through IVF. Our strict protocols minimize any risks.

**8. What is the right age to consider IVF?**
If you are under 35 and have tried naturally for a year, or over 35 and have tried for 6 months without success, you should consult an IVF specialist. Fertility declines sharply after age 35.

**9. What is the difference between IVF and IUI?**
In IUI (Intrauterine Insemination), sperm is placed directly into the uterus, and fertilization happens naturally inside the body. In IVF, eggs are retrieved and fertilized with sperm in a laboratory, and the resulting embryo is placed into the uterus.

**10. What tests are needed before starting IVF?**
Standard pre-IVF tests include AMH (to check ovarian reserve), a pelvic ultrasound, infectious disease screening, semen analysis for the male partner, and a complete blood count.

**11. How long does one IVF cycle take?**
An IVF cycle typically takes about 3 to 4 weeks, starting from the first day of your period to the embryo transfer.

**12. Can male infertility be treated with IVF?**
Yes, advanced IVF combined with ICSI (Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection) is highly effective for severe male infertility, even in cases of very low sperm count or poor motility.

**13. What is blastocyst transfer?**
A blastocyst is an embryo that has been cultured in the lab for 5 days. Transferring a blastocyst (rather than a Day-3 embryo) significantly increases implantation success rates.

**14. Are there any side effects of IVF injections?**
Mild side effects like bloating, mild cramping, or breast tenderness are common. Severe complications like Ovarian Hyperstimulation Syndrome (OHSS) are rare with our modern, carefully monitored protocols.

**15. Can I choose the gender of the baby during IVF?**
No. In India, gender selection (sex determination) before or after conception is strictly prohibited by law (PCPNDT Act).

**16. Does insurance cover IVF treatment in Delhi?**
Some corporate health insurance policies are beginning to cover fertility treatments. We recommend checking with your specific insurance provider.

**17. Do I need complete bed rest after embryo transfer?**
No, complete bed rest is a myth and not scientifically proven to improve success. We recommend 1-2 days of relative rest, after which you can resume normal, light activities.

**18. What happens to extra embryos?**
If you have multiple high-quality embryos, the extra ones can be vitrified (flash-frozen) and safely stored for future use, saving you from undergoing the entire stimulation process again.

**19. How do lifestyle habits affect IVF?**
Smoking, excessive alcohol, severe stress, and extreme obesity can negatively impact egg and sperm quality. A healthy lifestyle improves your chances of IVF success.

**20. Why do some IVF cycles fail?**
IVF can fail due to poor embryo quality (often related to age), poor uterine lining receptivity, or underlying chromosomal abnormalities in the embryo. We thoroughly investigate any failure before attempting the next cycle.
